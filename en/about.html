🌐 About the Illacme Press Publishing Team

Illacme Press is dedicated to breaking down digital barriers on centralized content platforms, fully restoring creative freedom and data physical sovereignty to every independent creator, scholar, and writing team.

🌟 Our Publishing Philosophy

1. Physical Sovereignty First

  • Your original manuscript (Markdown) remains permanently on your local disk; the system employs read-only probing and incremental synchronization.
  • We never lock into a specific format, ensuring perfect compatibility with standard exports from Obsidian, Logseq, Notion, and CommonMark.

2. Democratic Compute

  • Seamlessly schedules between local open-source small models (Ollama / LMStudio) and commercial large models (OpenAI / DeepSeek / Gemini / Baidu Qianfan, etc., with 29+ adapters).
  • Reduces translation compute costs by over 90% through paragraph-level incremental shadow caching (BlockShadowCache).

3. Write Once, Resonate Globally

  • A native-language manuscript is automatically polished, translated into multiple languages, generates SEO structured data, and simultaneously pushed to a global multi-channel matrix.
