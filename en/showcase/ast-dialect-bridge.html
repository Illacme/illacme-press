1. 💡 Smart Flattening of Obsidian Callout Blocks

  • Automatically recognizes 12 native Obsidian Callout syntaxes such as > [!NOTE], > [!TIP], > [!WARNING], and > [!DANGER];
  • Outputs semantic frosted glass containers for native themes, and outputs native Admonition containers for Docusaurus / VitePress, ensuring absolute visual consistency across all themes.

2. 📊 Lossless Compilation of Mermaid Topology Diagrams and LaTeX Complex Formulas

  • Intercepts Mermaid code blocks and $$ E=mc^2 $$ mathematical formulas within the manuscript, automatically handling escape characters and container wrapping;

3. 🔗 Dual-Link Translation with Absolute 404 Prevention

  • Precisely maps [Custom Alias](../../../../my document.html) in memory to the specific routing rules of the current target theme (e.g., showcase/my-document.html);
  • Deeply supports anchor jumps (e.g., [My Doc#Chapter 2](../../../../my doc.html#Chapter 2)) and multi-language automatic channel isolation, completely bidding farewell to 404 pages.
