🏷️ Multi-Brand Sovereignty Matrix

💡 Draft Once, Publish Independently Across a Diverse Matrix: Creators can establish fully independent publishing brands (Imprints) for different business lines, audiences, or language markets. All brands share the underlying core manuscript library but enjoy physically isolated binding themes, publishing modes, distribution channels, and brand compliance metadata.

🛠️ Core Mechanisms and Typical Scenarios

1. 📂 Physical-Level File Structure Isolation

The system delineates independent domain spaces for each brand under the imprints/{brand}/ directory:
* configs/config.imprint.yaml: Brand-specific exclusive configuration overrides, never polluting the global foundational base;
* themes/{theme}/: The theme runtime environment and static artifact output directory specific to this brand;
* metadata/: Independent site metadata, compliance declarations, and copyright owner information for this brand.

2. 🎭 Free-Form Combination Publishing Modes

Creators can freely enable or disable content channels for different brands:
* Technical Documentation Site (Docs Mode): Focused on product manuals, API references, and multi-level sidebar trees;
* Thoughts and Blogging Site (Blog Mode): Provides timelines, grid cards, and category tag retrieval;
* Product Showcase Gallery (Showcase Mode): Presents selected cases and multimedia galleries in full panorama;
* Standalone Homepage (Standalone Mode): A concise and sharp executive business card or brand landing page.

3. 🔄 Governance Center One-Click Instant Hot Switching

The current activated publishing brand can be freely switched from the top navigation bar of the governance center. The system automatically injects the corresponding brand context, binding preview, distribution strategy, and fingerprint ledger into the engine instantly, delivering an extremely smooth matrix management experience.
